How it works

Connected through the same standards your LMS already uses

HeyHi runs alongside your LMS. It connects through LTI 1.3 and the LMS's official APIs, the same way other approved learning tools do.

  1. Your IT team connects HeyHi

    An administrator registers HeyHi as an LTI 1.3 tool and approves a short, published list of permissions. No plugins in core, no code changes.

  2. You choose agents and rules

    Turn agents on by school, department or course. Set what each one may do on its own and what needs a teacher's approval.

  3. Agents work inside your courses

    Staff and students find HeyHi in the course menu. Approved grades, feedback and content go straight back into your LMS.

Security and data privacy

Your students' data stays yours, in your region and under your rules

We designed HeyHi Agentic LMS for schools whose first question is "where does our data go?" Here is the answer, in plain terms.

Data stays in region

Hosted in Singapore by default, with other regions available. Large institutions can choose a dedicated environment of their own.

Every school kept separate

Each institution has its own isolated data, encryption keys and search index. One school's data can never appear in another's.

Never used to train public AI

We use AI providers under zero-retention terms. Your data is not stored by them and is never used to train their models.

Only the access we need

HeyHi asks for the smallest set of LMS permissions the agents you enable require. Your IT team can review the full list before approving.

A complete audit trail

Every agent action is logged: who asked, which agent, what it did and who approved it. Logs can be exported for your reviews.

Your choice of AI model

Use leading AI models through private cloud connections, models hosted on your own servers, or HeyHi's own education model.

Your LMS→ Encrypted connection→ HeyHi, in your region→ Personal data removed where possible→ Private AI model, no retention Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. When your contract ends, your school's data is deleted on a schedule you agree.

Questions from school leaders

Common questions

Do we need to change or upgrade our LMS?

No. HeyHi connects through LTI 1.3 and your LMS's official APIs. Your administrator registers the tool and approves its permissions. Your LMS code, theme and data structure stay as they are.

Who can see our students' data?

Only your own staff, according to the roles they already have in your LMS. HeyHi staff access is limited to support you request, and every access is logged.

Will the AI make decisions about grades on its own?

No. Grading, messages to students and course changes are drafted for a teacher to review. Nothing reaches the gradebook or a student's inbox until someone approves it.

Which AI models do you use?

You choose. Options include leading AI models through private, zero-retention cloud connections, open models hosted in a dedicated environment, or HeyHi's own education model. Your choice can differ by school.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most schools begin with a pilot in one department or year level, using two or three agents. You can review results with us before rolling out further.

What does our IT team need to do?

Register HeyHi as an LTI 1.3 tool, approve the permission list and, for proactive agents, switch on your LMS's event feed. We guide your team through each step.

Start with a pilot in one department

See the agents working in your own LMS, with your own courses, under your own security rules. We will walk your leadership and IT teams through every step.
